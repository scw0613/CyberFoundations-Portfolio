**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
The cybersecurity landscape is one that is becoming more complicated each day. Many households now have multiple devices, from phones and tablets, to TVs, doorbell cameras, healthcare devices, even refrigerators. Keeping these devices is no longer a matter of convenience, it is a necessity. Someone could potentially breach a TV and then quietly lurk on a network, connecting data until gathering enough information to steal multiple family members identities. **How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**
```

```
At the simplest level, layers interact by processing millions of instructions per second. A left-click, right-click, and double-click are all different instructions that is meant to return a result that leads to more instructions being processed to open a file, save a change, or grant access.
```
